OverviewIntroduction

EnerTec FZC ("EnerTec," "we," "us," or "our"), a company registered in Sharjah Airport International Free Zone (SAIF Zone), Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website at enertecsc.com or engage with our services. Please read this policy carefully. By using our website or services, you consent to the practices described herein.

Scope: This policy applies to all information collected through our website, email communications, consultancy engagements, and any related services offered by EnerTec FZC.

02

Data CollectionInformation We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal and non-personal information:

Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and job title provided when you contact us or complete a form.
Inquiry Details: Information you provide in service inquiries, quote requests, or consultancy engagement forms.
Usage Data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs collected automatically via cookies and analytics tools.
Communications: Records of correspondence when you contact us by email or phone, including any attachments.
Business Information: Company registration details, project requirements, and technical specifications shared during consultancy engagements.

We do not collect sensitive personal data (such as health, financial account, or government ID information) unless explicitly required for a specific service engagement and with your prior consent.

03

PurposeHow We Use Your Information

EnerTec uses the information we collect for the following legitimate business purposes:

To respond to your inquiries, quote requests, and service engagement applications.
To deliver consultancy, training, engineering, and supply chain services you have requested.
To communicate service updates, industry insights, and relevant company news (where you have opted in).
To improve our website functionality, content, and user experience through analytics.
To comply with applicable UAE laws, regulations, and legal obligations.
To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or security breaches.

We will not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects without your explicit consent.

04

DisclosureInformation Sharing

EnerTec does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist in operating our website, conducting business, or servicing you, under strict confidentiality agreements.
Business Partners: Where your project requires collaboration with partner suppliers or sub-consultants, with your prior knowledge.
Legal Compliance: When required by UAE federal law, court order, or governmental authority.
Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as a business asset, subject to equivalent privacy protections.

All third parties with whom we share data are contractually required to handle it with at least the same level of protection as described in this policy.

05

ProtectionData Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

Secure HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted through our website.
Access controls ensuring only authorized personnel handle personal data.
Regular review of our data collection, storage, and processing practices.
Physical security measures at our offices in Sharjah, UAE.

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but commit to notifying you promptly in the event of a data breach that materially affects your rights.

Storage DurationData Retention
